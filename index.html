QuestionsFrequently asked
What is SCIVYT?

SCIVYT is an AI-powered education and research technology company. Its five platforms — Yetzu, Beentu AI, Submit Right, VITUOR, and Nationcite — support learning, academic writing, publishing, peer review, and research visibility as one connected ecosystem.

Who are SCIVYT platforms for?

Universities and institutions, researchers and authors, medical professionals, and learners — each platform operates for a specific stage of the academic lifecycle, from a learner's first curriculum to a researcher's global visibility.

How does SCIVYT combine AI with human expertise?

Through hybrid intelligence: AI systems scale the work — analysis, drafting support, verification, workflows — while human experts make the decisions that carry academic consequence: mentors who teach, editors who refine, reviewers who judge.

How can an institution partner with SCIVYT?

Write to support@scivyt.com or use the contact page to book a demo. The team works with institutions, publishers, and research organisations across three continents.
